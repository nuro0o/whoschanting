# Vigilante

The Vigilante is a Town role available in Custom games and the random Town pools in Chaos and Paranoia.

- Once per match, optionally shoot another living player at night. Keeping watch saves the shot.
- The target is eliminated at dawn. If the target's true allegiance is not Cult, the Vigilante also leaves the village with guilt and becomes a spectator.
- All other night actions resolve before shots, including chants. Shooting the final cultist wins for Town, even if the ritual fills that night.
- Curse protection does not stop a shot. Oracle veils and forged readings do not change guilt. Misdirection can change the target, and guilt follows the actual target.
- Dreamweaver disruption cancels the shot but still spends it, like other once-per-match abilities.
- Multiple shots resolve simultaneously. A Vigilante shot by another Vigilante still fires their submitted, undisrupted shot; being shot takes precedence over leaving with guilt in their status.

Public dawn messages report who was shot and who left with guilt. Private journal results and the final recap record each shot's outcome. Eliminated players cannot act or vote, and rematches reset the shot and elimination status.

No `.env` changes or database migrations are required.
