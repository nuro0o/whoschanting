# Unlockable character portrait art

Tool: built-in `image_gen__imagegen` (four separate generation calls). No CLI or API key used.

Style was informed by visual inspection of `public/assets/chanting/characters-reimagined-illustrated.png`. These are four original portraits, generated with the prompts below; no source faces were repurposed. Each resulting PNG is 1254 × 1254 pixels. All four inspected for subject, framing, anatomy, palette, and no text/borders. Originals remain under the built-in generated-images directory.

## tidecaller

Final asset: `public/assets/chanting/unlocks/tidecaller.png`

Exact prompt:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: single square 1024x1024 unlockable character portrait for the coastal social deduction game Whos Chanting.
Create ONE new original person, chest-up, face large and clearly readable at small avatar size. Style: textured painterly storybook character illustration with broad angular brush strokes, subtle ink outlines, earthy warm skin tones, expressive knowing suspicious faces, muted bluegreen harbor scenery, brass and gold highlights, loosely painted misty maritime architecture. Refined illustrated board-game art with attractive characterful faces and realistic adult proportions, not photorealistic, not glossy 3D, not cartoon mascot. Face and full hairstyle sit in the central upper two-thirds, prop near lower chest, comfortably inside the square crop.
Lighting: warm amber on face, cool foggy harbor fill; quiet, mysterious, wry personality.
Constraints: exactly one portrait, one person, edge-to-edge painted background, no panel grid, no lettering, no watermark, no decorative border, no team insignia, no transparent checkerboard.
Subject: The Tidecaller: a weathered older woman with sea-weathered fair freckled skin, abundant salt-white curls, a high-collared teal seafarer's coat with a small brass clasp. She holds a pearlescent spiral conch shell at her chest, one hand visible. Calm shrewd asymmetrical smile, strong cheekbones, distinctive white curly silhouette. A fogbound fishing harbor softly suggested behind her.
```
